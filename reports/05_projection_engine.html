Purpose¶

  • the baseline Lee-Carter parameters from Notebook 03;
  • the calibrated climate-sensitivity parameter $\lambda_{x,r}$ from Notebook 04; and
  • the projected composite climate index $C_{t,r}$ from Notebook 02.

The projection model is:

$\log m_{x,t,r}^{\text{climate-adjusted}}=a_{x,r}+b_x k_t^{\text{proj}}+\lambda_{x,r} C_{t,r}^{\text{proj}}$

For comparison, the no-climate baseline projection is:

$\log m_{x,t,r}^{\text{baseline}}=a_{x,r}+b_x k_t^{\text{proj}}$

The climate component is therefore:

$\Delta \log m_{x,t,r}=\lambda_{x,r} C_{t,r}^{\text{proj}}$

For this case-study implementation, $\lambda_{x,r}$ is floored at zero when used in projection. This avoids interpreting a positive heat-related climate index as reducing projected mortality. This is a modeling choice and should be reviewed as part of sensitivity testing and model governance.
